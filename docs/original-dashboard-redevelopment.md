# Original dashboard redevelopment scope

Extend the original `App.tsx` dashboard. Abandon the recently added CommerceBoard interface as the product direction. Preserve original targets, commission controls, imports, history, company/brand views and order-detail flows.

- Rename the business module; retain external procurement as an order category.
- Classify self-incubated orders by VP first: ten-thirty and Chengdu, then ordinary platform categories. Verify the original source fields.
- Publish, deal and payment use their corresponding event dates. External published orders do not require payment.
- Keep actual revenue attribution based on order ownership. Target allocation ratios do not divide actual revenue. Retain the existing total revenue logic pending a separate business decision.
- Import monthly publication targets with workbook, sheet, cell, raw-value and rounding provenance. Exclude integrated marketing and the separate business-development special block.
- Expand the original dashboard roster and server permissions to all authorized developers and related customer staff; special cross-department visibility preserves the real organization.
- Customer groups and developer/customer relationships are distinct. Validate both against private source documents.
- Add official-calendar Fangpian weeks to the original monthly interface; weekly targets remain blank.
- Use shared, rate-limited, resumable collection and atomic successful-batch publication. Aim for fresh data visible at noon and 18:00; show actual completion and source-read times, including delays.
- Repair original company/brand phase filters, complete-result order-count sorting, yuan/wan and decimal controls, company/brand amount shares and per-order drilldowns.
- Improve spacing and constrained-height tables with sticky headers. Preserve the original navigation and interaction structure.
- Verify fixed private order samples, target cells, period boundaries, pagination, exact amounts, role isolation, original feature regression, real personal SSO and actual scheduled refresh outcomes before cutover.

The backup does not switch production. Full identifiable requirements, source evidence and deployment/data backups remain private. Public demonstration values must not be used for business acceptance.
